Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about app landing pages

What is an app landing page?

It is a single web page that introduces an app, shows what it does with clear pictures, builds trust, and asks the visitor to download or sign up.

What makes an app landing page good?

A clear promise at the top, real product pictures, simple words, customer reviews, one strong button, and a fast layout that works well on a phone.

Why design for an Indian audience?

Many people browse on mid-range phones and slow networks. Light pages with friendly words and familiar examples work best.

Can I use these ideas in my own project?

Yes. Use the layouts as inspiration, then add your own words, colours and pictures so the page feels like yours.

How long does a reply take?
